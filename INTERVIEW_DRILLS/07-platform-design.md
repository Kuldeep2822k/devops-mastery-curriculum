# Platform Design — Drills

Answer in: assumptions → design → failure modes → observability → rollback/safety → tradeoffs.

## Questions

- What is a “golden path” and how do you measure adoption?
- How do you design guardrails that don’t block teams (policy as code, paved roads)?
- How do you handle multi-tenancy and RBAC in a shared platform?
- What is your strategy for platform reliability (SLOs, incident response, ownership)?
