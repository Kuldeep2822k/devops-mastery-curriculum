# Observability + SRE — Drills

Answer in: symptoms → signals → diagnosis plan → mitigation → prevention.

## Questions

- What is your alerting philosophy (page vs ticket) and how do you reduce alert noise?
- Design SLOs for an API with a queue-based backend. What do you alert on?
- Walk through an incident where dashboards were misleading. How do you validate signals?
- How do you choose retries/timeouts/circuit breaking to avoid cascading failures?
