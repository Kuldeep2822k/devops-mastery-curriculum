# IaC + Config Management — Drills

Answer in: assumptions → design → safety → drift → rollback → tradeoffs.

## Questions

- How do you manage Terraform state safely for a team? What are your failure drills?
- Describe how you detect and handle drift without turning Terraform into “delete-recreate”.
- How do you structure Ansible inventories/vars to avoid outages and enable safe rollouts?
- Describe an incident caused by configuration drift and how you’d prevent recurrence.
