# Checklist — Capstone 02: Platform as Product

- [ ] Scope and constraints documented (what you will not do)
- [ ] Top risks and failure domains identified
- [ ] At least 3 incident drills executed with postmortems
- [ ] Runbooks + ADRs complete and internally consistent
- [ ] Self-grade and list 5 improvement actions
