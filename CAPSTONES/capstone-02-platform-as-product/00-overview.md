# Capstone 02: Platform as Product

## Goal

Deliver a staff-level outcome: stable operations, clear interfaces, and documented tradeoffs.

## Required Artifacts

- system diagram and assumptions
- runbooks for top 5 incidents
- ADRs for top 3 tradeoffs
- incident drill logs + postmortems
- evidence bundle (commands/outputs)
