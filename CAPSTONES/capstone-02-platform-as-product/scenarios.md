# Scenarios — Capstone 02: Platform as Product

- A deploy causes partial outage with mixed versions
- A key rotates and breaks background workers
- A dependency degrades slowly causing saturation and timeouts
- A cost spike forces you to choose safety vs spend
- A security incident requires containment and customer comms
