# Review Questions — Module 07 Delivery

Answer from memory first.

1. Explain “build once, promote many”. What breaks if you rebuild in each environment?
2. Compare rolling vs canary vs blue/green. Give a scenario where each is the best choice.
3. What are rollback triggers? Give three measurable triggers and how you verify recovery.
4. Why do rollbacks fail due to migrations? Explain expand/contract in plain language.
5. What signals do you watch during a canary? Which are leading vs lagging indicators?
6. What is the difference between containment and fix during an incident deploy?
7. In Kubernetes, why does readiness matter for delivery safety?
8. What evidence must be captured before you roll back a production deploy?
9. How do feature flags reduce delivery risk? What new risks do they introduce?
10. What belongs in release metadata for incident forensics?
11. How can “approval gates” help and how can they hurt?
12. Write a 6-step canary promotion checklist.
