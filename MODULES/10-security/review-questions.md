# Review Questions — Module 10 Security

1. What are the top 5 assets in a DevOps delivery system?
2. What are the top 5 ways secrets leak? How do you prevent each?
3. What is least privilege and why is it an incident control?
4. Why is CI a high-value target? What controls reduce risk?
5. What is a supply-chain attack? Give three examples.
6. Why is `:latest` risky? How do digests and pinning help?
7. What is an SBOM and why is it useful during incident response?
8. What should your “secret leaked” incident response steps be?
9. What are policy gates? Give three gates you would enforce.
10. How can security gates be designed to not destroy developer productivity?
11. What evidence must you capture before rotating secrets?
12. What belongs in an ADR about security controls in CI/CD?
