# Runbook Template

## Summary

- What is broken? Who is impacted? What is the blast radius?

## Detection

- Alerts, dashboards, log queries, symptoms

## Diagnosis

- Fast checks (confirm/deny hypotheses)
- Deep checks (when fast checks are inconclusive)

## Mitigation

- Safe actions first (reduce scope, revert, shed load)
- Rollback plan and triggers

## Recovery Validation

- What must be true to declare recovery?

## Follow-ups

- prevention, automation, documentation gaps, alert improvements
