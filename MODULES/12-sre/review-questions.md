# Review Questions — Module 12 SRE

1. What are the minimal incident roles and responsibilities?
2. What evidence should be captured before rollback/restart?
3. Define containment vs fix. When do you prefer rollback?
4. What makes a good postmortem action item?
5. Define toil. Give 5 examples in a real system.
6. How do error budgets change release behavior?
7. What are fast-burn vs slow-burn alerts?
8. What are common capacity failure patterns?
9. Why do retries without backoff cause outages?
10. What is “on-call sustainability” and how do you measure it?
11. What is a safe break-glass policy for overrides?
12. What belongs in an ADR about incident response policy?
