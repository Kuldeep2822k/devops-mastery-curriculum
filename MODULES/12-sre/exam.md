# Practical Exam — Module 12 SRE

## Rules

- Time-box: 120 minutes.
- No internet.
- Submit evidence: timelines, decisions, and verification signals.

## Tasks

### Task 1: Incident Drill

Requirements:

- run a simulated incident
- define roles and comms cadence
- capture a timeline with evidence
- contain and verify recovery over a window

Grading criteria:

- decisions justified by signals
- evidence preserved before changes

### Task 2: Postmortem

Requirements:

- write a postmortem with at least 5 prevention items
- assign owners and clear success criteria

Grading criteria:

- action items are concrete and reduce recurrence

### Task 3: SLO + Error Budget Policy

Requirements:

- define SLI/SLO for a service
- define burn-rate alert policy
- define release gating rules based on budget status

Grading criteria:

- policy is explicit and actionable

### Task 4: Runbook + ADR

Requirements:

- Runbook: “service down / high latency” with first 10 minute steps and containment.
- ADR: choose an incident response policy or error budget policy and justify tradeoffs.
