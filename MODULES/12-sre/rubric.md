# Rubric — Module 12 SRE

Use global rubric meanings: [Evidence and Rubrics](../../00-HOW-TO-USE/04-evidence-rubrics.md)

## Skill 1: Incident Management

- 0: No structure; no timeline; guesswork.
- 1: Some structure but weak evidence preservation and comms.
- 2: Clear roles, timeline, containment, verification.
- 3: Fast diagnosis and safe decisions under pressure; reduces blast radius.
- 4: Builds org-wide incident process and drills; consistently reduces MTTR.

## Skill 2: SLOs and Error Budgets

- 0: No measurable SLOs.
- 1: SLOs exist but not used for decisions.
- 2: SLOs tied to alerts and release policy.
- 3: Burn-rate alerts and clear gating rules; stakeholder alignment.
- 4: Drives reliability governance across teams using error budgets.

## Skill 3: Toil Reduction

- 0: Repetitive manual work accepted as normal.
- 1: Can name toil but no systematic reduction.
- 2: Identifies and automates key toil; improves runbooks.
- 3: Builds safe automation with guardrails and audits.
- 4: Sustained toil reduction program; improves on-call quality.

## Skill 4: Capacity and Resilience

- 0: No capacity model; outages predictable.
- 1: Reactive scaling only.
- 2: Basic capacity planning and resilience patterns understood.
- 3: Uses measurements and load models; avoids retry storms and performance cliffs.
- 4: Designs reliability posture with clear headroom and failure domain thinking.
