# Review Questions — Module 11 Observability

1. When do you use logs vs metrics vs traces for an incident?
2. What makes an alert actionable? List 5 required fields.
3. What is cardinality and why does it matter for metrics cost and reliability?
4. Define SLI, SLO, and error budget in plain language.
5. What is a burn rate alert and why does it reduce noise?
6. What are the “golden signals” and why are they useful?
7. Why should dashboards include version markers and deploy annotations?
8. What evidence should you capture before rollback/restart?
9. What are common ways logs become useless during incidents?
10. How do you design a dashboard for on-call vs for product owners?
11. Give two examples of bad alerts and how to improve them.
12. What belongs in an ADR about alerting policy or SLO definition?
